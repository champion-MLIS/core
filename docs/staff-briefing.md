# Staff Briefing — Coordinating Guest Follow-Up

*A short, copy-and-send note for the staff member building the visitor workflow, and for Becky. Adapt freely — it's written in a warm, collaborative register so the de-confliction conversation starts from "let's complement each other," not "stop what you're doing."*

---

## Three-sentence version of MLIS (for anyone)

Champion has been quietly building a system called MLIS that automatically follows up with every first-time guest — it watches Planning Center for new connect cards, then runs a 21-day sequence of personal texts, emails, and prompts in Champion's voice, with a real staff member approving every message before it sends. It's built so no guest ever falls through the cracks and so our team is freed from the administrative side of follow-up to focus on the personal side. Pastoral care and human judgment stay in human hands at every step — the system drafts and reminds; people decide and connect.

---

## Suggested note to send the staff member

> Hey [name] —
>
> I want to loop you in on something before our two efforts drift into each other, because I think they're actually a great fit together.
>
> I've been building a system (we're calling it MLIS) that handles the **digital follow-up** for first-time guests — the texts and emails over the first three weeks, drafted in our voice, with a person approving each one before it goes out. It keys off the "New Here" connect card in Planning Center.
>
> Your visitor workflow and this could collide if we're not careful — the worst outcome would be a guest getting contacted twice by two different systems and feeling processed instead of welcomed. So I'd love to grab 30 minutes and make sure we're dividing the work cleanly.
>
> My instinct on the split: **MLIS takes the digital follow-up after Sunday** (the texts/emails/sequence), and **the visitor workflow owns everything that happens in the building on Sunday** — the welcome desk, name tags, the gift, walking families to kids' check-in, the warm in-person handoff. That in-person layer is the part a system can never do, and it's where your work matters most.
>
> Nothing you've built is wasted — I just want to make sure we're two halves of one warm welcome, not two machines pinging the same guest. Can we find time this week?
>
> — Stephen

## The one-sentence division to land on

Once you've talked, write down the agreed line so it's unambiguous. Recommended:

> **"MLIS handles every digital touch to a guest after Sunday. The visitor team handles everything that happens in the building on Sunday. Neither contacts a guest the other is also contacting."**
