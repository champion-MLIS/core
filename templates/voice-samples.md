# Champion Voice — Approved Samples

These are reference samples for every communication this system produces. Every agent drafts against these. If a draft doesn't feel like one of these, it doesn't send.

---

## The Standard

> *"We'd love to invite you to Starting Point — a relaxed conversation where we can hear your story, answer questions, and help you find your place at Champion Church."*

This sentence, written by Pastor Stephen, is the voice benchmark. Every communication should feel like it came from the same person who wrote this.

**What makes it work:**
- "We'd love to" — warm invitation, zero pressure
- "relaxed conversation" — disarms formality
- "hear your story" — it's about them, not us
- "help you find your place" — assumes they belong, just haven't found it yet

## Guest Follow-Up — First Visit

**Email**

Subject: Great to meet you, [First Name] 👋

---

[First Name],

So glad you were with us Sunday. Whether it was your first time at Champion or you've been thinking about checking us out for a while — we're really glad you made it.

[Sermon reference if available — e.g., "Hope the message on [topic] resonated with you."]

We'd love to stay connected. Here are a few things that might interest you:

[Relevant ministry link 1]
[Relevant ministry link 2 — only if applicable, e.g., kids' ministry if children attended]

No pressure, no ask — just wanted you to know there's a place here for you.

See you soon,
Champion Church

---

**SMS — short enough that it reads like a friend texting, not a marketing blast. Aim for ~200 characters or under.**

Hey [First Name]! So glad you joined us Sunday. We'd love to stay connected — champion.church has everything you need. See you soon! 🙌

## Starting Point Invitation

**Email**

Subject: An invitation just for you, [First Name]

---

[First Name],

We've loved having you at Champion. As you settle in, we'd love to get to know you a little better.

We'd love to invite you to Starting Point — a relaxed conversation where we can hear your story, answer questions, and help you find your place at Champion Church.

No agenda. No pressure. Just a chance to connect.

Interested? Reply to this email or reach us at [contact]. We'll find a time that works for you.

Can't wait to hear your story,
Champion Church

## Voice Rules — Quick Reference

| ✅ Say This | ❌ Never Say This |
|------------|-----------------|
| "We'd love to..." | "You must..." |
| "No pressure" | "You need to..." |
| "There's a place for you" | "Don't miss out" |
| "Whenever you're ready" | "You're falling behind" |
| "We miss you" | "You haven't been..." |
| "Hear your story" | "We need you to..." |
| Faith-filled, expectant | Sin-focused, obligation-based |
| Warm, personal | Corporate, formal |
| First name always | "Dear Friend" or "Valued Member" |

---

## Tone Test

Before any communication sends, run it through this three-question test:

1. **Does it sound like a person wrote it?** (Not software, not a form letter)
2. **Would someone feel welcomed or pressured after reading it?** (Welcomed only)
3. **Could Pastor Stephen read this from the platform and it sound like him?** (Yes = ship it)

If any answer is wrong, rewrite it.
